# Schenk-von-Limpurg-Gymnasium: visual references

Research checked on 4 September 2026. The game is a stylized, playable interpretation of the school in Gaildorf, not a surveyed reconstruction. Public photos were visually inspected; no measured floor plans, room dimensions, or complete circulation plan were available in the sources reviewed.

## Supporting context

- The school's [Profil page](https://www.svlg-gaildorf.de/de/unsere-schule/profil) explicitly identifies the atrium as a lunchtime common area and places the cafeteria in the school center.
- Its [Open House page](https://www.svlg-gaildorf.de/de/unsere-schule/tag-der-offenen-tuer) describes tours of the renovated school and use of the atrium as a café.
- The project architect's [renovation page](https://www.kraft-sha.de/schulsanierung-schenk-von-limpurg-gymnasium-und-schloss-realschule/) describes new interior/exterior color and material concepts for both the Gymnasium and the neighboring Realschule. Its lead image is explicitly named **Realschule**, so it was not used as evidence for this school's exterior.

## Translation into the game

Use the atrium's glazed pitched roof, grey structure, yellow accents, white brick and dark tiled galleries as the strongest identifying features. A stair hall with yellow bench/table groupings and teal courtyard glazing provides a second recognizable environment. Ribbon-window classroom wings and lawn establish the exterior.

The race route, room connections, building dimensions, obstacle arrangements, accessible ramps, classroom furniture, chalkboard content, fictional signage and projector modifications are game design choices. Any modeled classroom is an interpretation: the photographs above do not establish its interior arrangement. A racing ramp is not evidence of an actual ramp in the school. The sources do not establish how these photographed spaces connect into a complete floor plan, nor what the school looked like during the player's school years.

The original photographs are references only and are not included as game textures or redistributed assets. No reusable image license was established; rights remain with their respective owners. The game geometry and procedural materials are original. No student likenesses, personal records, or identifying classroom notices are reproduced.

## Expanded user-supplied references and track redesign

The September 2026 polish pass also inspected the fourteen photographs/map screenshots supplied directly in the conversation. Images 12 and 13 show the same stair-hall view. These references add details that were missing from the first room-based prototype:

- The satellite and OpenStreetMap views show a long, articulated school complex, paved forecourts, adjoining school buildings, Schlosspark and its pond to the south-west, and the Kocher along the park edge. They support a campus-and-park setting, rather than three enclosed atrium arenas. Track lengths, widths, turn radii and connections are still game design, not surveyed paths.
- The exterior photographs show white school wings with long teal window bands, projecting upper window bays, blue exposed drainpipes, an elevated classroom volume above open ground, and a courtyard lawn with a flowering tree.
- The entrance/passage images show a flat dark canopy on red columns, a circular upper window, paved planting beds, railings around a sunken lightwell, and barrel-vault bicycle shelters on timber posts.
- The interior images establish the graphite floor, yellow doors, gallery radiators, grey railings, glazed roof, substantial yellow stair stringers and slab benches. The group photograph contributes only its visible atrium architecture; people and classroom displays were not reproduced.
- Some exterior screenshots explicitly show photographs from 2018. The scenery combines these architectural references with the more recent interior imagery; it does not assert that every detail coexisted at one date.

**Atrium Circuit** stays inside the glass-roof hall around a central stair/gallery structure. **Canopy Run** travels through separate school wings, the red-column covered passage, courtyards and bicycle shelters. **Kocher Park** follows an outdoor park circuit around a pond and along a guarded riverbank, returning through the school forecourt. These are distinct modeled spaces and routes, not alternate furniture arrangements in one room.

No map tiles or supplied photographs are packaged as game textures. Procedural geometry uses the broad architectural and landscape observations above. No real school security systems, private spaces, or personal information are modeled.
